    About Myth

    Cinematographer, editor & colourist

    I've been doing this for about four years. I started out making social media content for a small brand, which gave me a very real insight into what's out there.

    From there I specialised in commercials, music videos, documentary and vertical work, and realised I wanted to be a filmmaker: to tell stories bigger than just a passing scroll.
